Research Paper

The Role of Corporate Disclosure Specificity in Cybersecurity Risk and Predictive Modeling

A first-author project on whether more specific SEC risk disclosure helps predict future cyber incidents.

Request details Back

Input

SEC 10-K risk-factor text focused on cybersecurity disclosure.

Method

NER-based specificity measures with logistic and tree-based rare-event models.

Result

F1 performance above 0.20, versus a prior benchmark near 0.06.
