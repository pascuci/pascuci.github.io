Working Paper in Progress

Managerial Evasion in Earnings Call Q&A and Capital Market Information Processing

A transcript-based project on whether evasive managerial answers weaken information transfer.

Request details Back

Question

Do evasive answers in earnings-call Q&A reduce the market's ability to process information?

Data

3.4 million question-answer pairs linked with Compustat, CRSP, and IBES.

Measure

An evasion score built from the Eva-4B-V2 / EvasionBench framework.

Early Signal

Higher evasion is associated with more forecast error, more dispersion, and higher crash risk.
